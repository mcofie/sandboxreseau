## 📌 #manifesto

```
# 🇬🇭 Sandbox Réseau

**A network for builders who ship.**

We're engineers, designers, and makers in Accra building the next wave of products from Ghana. This isn't a networking group. It's a workspace.

---

## Our Values

**1. Signal over Noise**
Every message should add value. No fluff, no "thoughts?" without context, no self-promotion disguised as conversation.

**2. Progress over Polish**
Share your messy V1. Post the broken prototype. We'd rather see your WIP than wait for your press release.

**3. Learn in Public**
Document your process. Write the tutorial you wish existed. Your failures are someone else's roadmap.

---

## What Happens Here

- Builders demo real projects (not pitch decks).
- Engineers share problems and get async code reviews.
- Designers post work-in-progress for brutal, honest feedback.
- Founders find technical co-founders who actually ship.

---

> 🛠️ **Ready?** Head to #rules to understand how we operate, then #start-here to get verified.
```

## 📜 #rules

```
# 📜 Community Rules

These aren't suggestions. They're how we keep signal high and noise low.

---

## The Rules

**1. No "Hello" Messages**
Don't send `Hello` and wait. State your question or context in the same message. We're async-first.

❌ "Hey, anyone here?"
✅ "Anyone worked with Paystack webhooks in Next.js? Getting 500s on verification—here's my route: [link]"

**2. No Unsolicited DMs**
Ask permission in a public channel first. Cold DMs for sales, "collaboration," or recruitment = instant ban.

**3. No Low-Effort Promo**
You can share your work, but lead with the story: what you built, how, what you learned. Drop a link with zero context = message deleted.

**4. Give Before You Take**
Answer questions. Review someone's PR. Share a resource. Build reputation before asking for favors.

**5. Respect the Craft**
Critique the work, not the person. Be direct, not dismissive. Junior devs asking "obvious" questions are welcome—we all started somewhere.

---

## Consequences

- **First offense:** Warning + message removed.
- **Second offense:** 24-hour mute.
- **Third offense:** Permanent ban, no appeal.

Egregious violations (spam, harassment, unsolicited DMs) = immediate ban.

---

> ✅ Agree? Head to #start-here to verify and introduce yourself.
```

```
# 🏆 Showcase

This is where you share **shipped work**. Not ideas. Not "coming soon." Shipped.

---

## Posting Guidelines

Use this format:

**Project:** [Name]
**What it does:** [1-2 sentences]
**Stack:** [Technologies used]
**Link:** [Live URL or repo]
**Lesson learned:** [One thing you'd do differently]

---

### Example:

**Project:** GatePass
**What it does:** QR-based event check-in system for Accra meetups. Handles 500+ guests with offline sync.
**Stack:** Next.js, Supabase, Paystack, Vercel
**Link:** gatepass.app
**Lesson learned:** Should've added bulk import earlier—organizers needed it from day one.

---

## Rules

- ✅ Shipped products, MVPs, side projects, open-source tools
- ✅ Honest post-mortems and lessons
- ❌ "Check out my startup" with no context
- ❌ Vaporware or landing pages with no product behind them

---

> 🛠️ Build it. Ship it. Share it here.
```

## 🔍 #code-review

```
# 🔍 Code Review

Async code reviews from the community. Post your PR, get feedback.

---

## How to Ask for a Review

**Language/Framework:** [e.g., TypeScript, React, Python]
**What it does:** [Brief context]
**What I need feedback on:** [Specific areas — architecture, performance, naming, etc.]
**Link:** [GitHub PR, Gist, or code snippet]

---

### Example:

**Language/Framework:** Next.js + Supabase
**What it does:** Webhook handler for Paystack payment verification
**What I need feedback on:** Error handling flow—not sure if I should retry failed verifications or just log them.
**Link:** github.com/kofi/gatepass/pull/42

---

## Reviewer Guidelines

- Be specific. "This is bad" helps no one.
- Suggest alternatives, not just problems.
- Assume good intent—they're learning, like you.

---

> 🧑‍💻 Review others' code. You'll learn as much as they do.
```

```
**Problem:** [One sentence]
**What I've tried:** [List what you've already done]
**Stack:** [Relevant technologies]
**Error/Screenshot:** [Paste the error or attach an image]
**Code:** [Minimal reproducible snippet, or link to Gist/repo]

**Problem:** Supabase RLS policy blocking authenticated users from reading their own data.

**Stack:** Next.js, Supabase, TypeScript
**Error:** "new row violates row-level security policy"
**Code:** https://gist.github.com/kofi/abc123

---

## Answering Questions

- Answer if you know.
- Don't reply just to say "Google it."
- If you solved it, post your solution for future searchers.

---

> 🔧 Help someone today. You'll need it tomorrow.
```
