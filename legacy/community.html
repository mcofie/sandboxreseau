Community Guidelines

  • 01. Be kind and constructive. We're all here to grow. Offer feedback generously and receive it graciously.
  • 02. Credit work. When sharing resources or ideas, always attribute the original creators.
  • 03. Respect time. Be punctual to events, keep discussions on-topic, and value others' attention.
  • 04. No spam or pitches. Promotional content should add genuine value. No cold sales tactics.
  • 05. Keep it confidential. What's shared in the community stays in the community unless explicitly public.
